📤 Deploy DWx Traffic Manager to SharePoint

Step 1: Open SharePoint Site

Click the link below to open your SharePoint site:

https://hallofd.sharepoint.com/sites/DWxTrafficManager

Step 2: Create Document Library

  1. Click + New in the top menu
  2. Select Document library
  3. Name it: DWxTrafficManagerApp
  4. Click Create

Step 3: Upload Files

Open File Explorer and navigate to:

c:\Projects\DWx-Traffic-Manager\dist

Upload these files to the DWxTrafficManagerApp library:

Root level:
  • index.html
  • vite.svg
Create 'assets' folder, then upload:
  • assets/index-aQzcD1n_.js
  • assets/index-W_onuapF.css

(You can skip the .map file - it's for debugging only)

Step 4: Test the App

After uploading, visit:

https://hallofd.sharepoint.com/sites/DWxTrafficManager/DWxTrafficManagerApp/index.html

✅ After Upload Complete

Once uploaded, go to Teams Admin Center to upload the Teams app package:

  1. Go to admin.teams.microsoft.com
  2. Navigate to Teams apps → Manage apps
  3. Click + Upload new app
  4. Select: DWx-Traffic-Manager/DWxTrafficManager.zip
