Role Overview

Account Manager

You are the front door to Digital Workplace. Browse services, submit requests for your clients, and track deals through the sales pipeline.

Permissions
1
Browse

Explore Services & Products

Visit the Service Catalog to browse 6 categories or the Product Catalog to see all 29 DWx offerings. Each service has a detail page with included items, benefits, engagement phases, and ideal-fit profiles.

2
Create

Submit a Service or Product Request

Complete the 5-step wizard:

  • Select Service — pick from the catalog
  • Client Info — new or existing client details
  • Requirements & Budget — interest level, deal value, probability, timeline
  • Proposed Slots — suggest up to 3 discovery meeting times
  • Review & Submit — confirm and send
3
Wait

Manager Reviews & Assigns Specialist

Your request enters the pipeline as a Lead. A manager will review it, qualify the opportunity, and assign a pre-sales specialist. You'll receive an email when the specialist is assigned.

4
Confirmed

Discovery Meeting Scheduled

The manager confirms one of your proposed time slots. A Teams meeting invite is sent to you, the specialist, and the client contact. The request moves to the Discovery stage.

5
Track

Monitor Progress in My Requests

Track your request through the funnel: Lead → Qualified → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost. Edit deal values, update requirements, and add comments at any time.

6
Outcome

Deal Won or Lost

When the deal closes, you'll receive a notification with the outcome. Won deals update your client's lifetime value. Lost deals capture the reason for future learning.

You Can

  • Browse services and products
  • Submit service and product requests
  • View and track your own requests
  • Edit deal value and probability
  • Update requirements and comments
  • Upload supporting documents

You Cannot

  • Assign or reassign specialists
  • Confirm discovery meeting slots
  • Transition pipeline stages
  • Access the Manager Dashboard
  • Access the Admin Panel
  • View other AMs' requests
