Role Overview

Manager

You are the command centre of Digital Workplace pre-sales. Assign specialists, confirm meetings, transition pipeline stages, and monitor KPIs across the entire team.

1
Receive

New Request Lands in Your Queue

When an Account Manager submits a service or product request, you receive an email alert and the request appears in the Sales Funnel → Request Queue tab. Review client details, interest level, and deal value.

2
Qualify

Validate & Qualify the Lead

Open the request and assess fit. Move the request from Lead → Qualified. You can also mark it as Lost if the opportunity isn't viable.

3
Assign

Assign a Pre-Sales Specialist

Open the Assign Specialist dialog. View each specialist's current deal count, role, and specializations. Pick the best fit. Both the AM and specialist receive notifications.

4
Confirm

Confirm a Discovery Meeting Slot

The AM proposed up to 3 time slots. Select one and confirm. The system:

  • Creates a Teams meeting on the shared calendar
  • Sends invites to the AM, specialist, and client contact
  • Moves the request to Discovery stage
  • Auto-creates a Session Preparation record for the specialist
5
Monitor

Track the Pipeline

Use the Sales Funnel Dashboard to monitor requests through Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation. Transition stages, update deal values, and keep the pipeline moving.

6
Close

Mark Deals as Won or Lost

When a deal closes, transition to Won (updates client lifetime value, decrements specialist deal count) or Lost (captures reason, cleans up calendar events). Both trigger notifications.

7
Administer

Manage Configuration & Master Data

Use the Admin Panel to manage team members, account managers, clients, services, specialists, and more. Import data via XLSX, provision SharePoint lists, and control manager access.

You Can

  • View all service and product requests
  • Assign and reassign specialists
  • Confirm discovery meeting slots
  • Transition pipeline stages (Lead to Won/Lost)
  • Update deal values and probabilities
  • Access Sales Funnel and Manager Dashboards
  • Manage all Admin Panel tabs (10 tabs)
  • Import clients and services via XLSX
  • Provision SharePoint lists
  • Control manager access for other users
  • Invite external guest Account Managers
  • Confirm product demo/trial slots

You Cannot

  • Submit requests on behalf of an AM
  • Edit another AM's personal details
  • Delete SharePoint lists (only provision)
  • Override audit log entries
  • Access Azure AD outside app scope
  • Modify production environment config
