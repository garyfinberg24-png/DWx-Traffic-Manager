✓ Advantages of Large Modal

  • User stays on catalog — can close and browse another service instantly
  • No full page navigation / back button needed
  • Progressive disclosure: quick view → full details → request
  • Faster browsing when comparing multiple services
  • Sidebar with quick facts always visible while scrolling content
  • Familiar pattern from e-commerce and SaaS product catalogs

✗ Trade-offs

  • Less screen real estate than a full page (900px vs full width)
  • Can't bookmark or share a direct URL to service details
  • Two modal layers could feel complex for simple services
  • Content needs tighter design to fit 900px width

💡 Recommendation

Use the large modal as the primary detail view. Remove the full page route (/services/:serviceId) entirely, or keep it as a fallback for direct URL access/bookmarks. The quick-view modal's "View Full Details" button opens the large modal instead of navigating to a new page. This creates a smooth, uninterrupted browsing experience within the catalog.
