General
What is DWx Traffic Manager? ▼
DWx Traffic Manager is a Microsoft Teams application designed for Digital Workplace's pre-sales coordination. It helps Account Managers create service requests, track deals through a sales funnel (Lead to Won/Lost), and coordinate with technical specialists for client engagements. The app integrates with SharePoint, Microsoft Graph, and Azure OpenAI for intelligent content generation.
Who can use the application? ▼
Account Managers, Business Development Managers, and the Sales Team can create and manage service requests. Managers have additional access to the sales pipeline dashboard, approval queue, and admin panel for system configuration.
How do I get started with DWx Traffic Manager? ▼
Simply log in with your Microsoft 365 account. From the landing page, browse available services in the Service Catalog, then click "New Request" to submit a service request. You can track all your requests under "My Requests" in the navigation bar.
Process
What are the stages in the sales funnel? ▼
The sales funnel has 7 stages: Lead, Qualified, Discovery, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, and Lost. Each stage has specific entry triggers and exit criteria. Deals progress from Lead through each stage as they mature, with the possibility of moving to Lost at any point.
How do I qualify a lead? ▼
To qualify a lead, validate the client's genuine interest and budget. Ensure you have the decision-maker's contact details, understand the project timeline, and confirm the scope aligns with DWx service offerings. Once validated, a manager can advance the deal from Lead to Qualified status.

Q What is DWx Traffic Manager?

Q Who can use the application?

Q What are the stages in the sales funnel?
The sales funnel has 7 stages: Lead, Qualified, Discovery, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, and Lost.
Q How do I create a new service request?
Click "New Request" in the navigation bar, then follow the 5-step wizard: select a service, enter client details, specify requirements, propose meeting times, and review your request before submitting.
Q How do I prepare for a discovery meeting?
Use the Session Preparation feature available on confirmed Discovery-stage requests. It provides AI-generated client profiles, talking points, suggested resources, and a preparation checklist to ensure you're fully ready.
Q What currencies are supported?
All deal values are denominated in South African Rand (ZAR). This applies to deal values, proposals, pricing breakdowns, and all commercial metrics.
