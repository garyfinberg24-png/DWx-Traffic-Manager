Role Overview

Pre-Sales Specialist

You are the technical expert. When a manager assigns you to a deal, you prepare for and lead discovery meetings using AI-powered session preparation tools.

Permissions
1
Assigned

Manager Assigns You to a Deal

A manager selects you as the pre-sales specialist for a service or product request. You receive an email notification with the client name, service requested, requirements summary, and your role.

2
Scheduled

Discovery Meeting Confirmed

The manager confirms one of the proposed time slots. You receive a Teams meeting invite on the shared calendar with the AM, client contact, and yourself as attendees. A Session Preparation record is automatically created.

3
Prepare

Use AI-Powered Session Preparation

Open the Session Prep dialog from the request details. Click "Generate AI Content" to have Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o) create:

  • Client Profile — industry context, engagement history, key stakeholders
  • Talking Points — opening, discovery, value proposition, objection handling, closing
  • Suggested Resources — slide decks, case studies, datasheets, demo scripts
  • Meeting Agenda — time-boxed items with descriptions
4
Review

Customise & Complete the Checklist

Edit talking points, select relevant resources, adjust the agenda, and work through the preparation checklist. Mark items as complete: review client history, prepare demo environment, test equipment, and more.

5
Remind

24-Hour Reminder

If your preparation isn't marked as Ready 24 hours before the meeting, you'll receive a reminder email with a link directly to the prep dialog.

6
Deliver

Lead the Discovery Meeting

Join the Teams meeting armed with your AI-enhanced preparation. After the meeting, the manager transitions the request to Proposal stage and the deal continues through the pipeline.

Client Profile AI Generated

Industry context, company background, engagement history, key stakeholders, and strategic context for the meeting.

Talking Points AI Generated

Organised by category: opening, discovery questions, value propositions, objection handling, and closing. Editable inline.

Resources AI Generated

Suggested slide decks, case studies, datasheets, and demo scripts with relevance scores. Select the ones you need.

Meeting Agenda AI Generated

Time-boxed agenda items in a timeline view. Customise durations and descriptions to match your meeting style.

Preparation Checklist

Pre-meeting checklist items with completion tracking. Progress bar shows overall readiness.

You Can

  • View requests assigned to you
  • Open and use Session Preparation dialog
  • Generate AI content (client profile, talking points, resources, agenda)
  • Edit talking points and meeting agenda
  • Select resources for your meeting
  • Complete checklist items
  • Mark preparation as Ready
  • Join discovery meetings via Teams
  • Browse service and product catalogs

You Cannot

  • Self-assign to requests
  • Submit service or product requests
  • Transition pipeline stages
  • Confirm or reschedule meeting slots
  • Access the Manager Dashboard
  • Access the Admin Panel
  • View other specialists' assignments
  • Modify client or service master data
