Hero Variations
  • V1 — Side-by-Side (~110px): Text on left, stats stacked vertically on right with divider. Keeps full sales copy visible. Best balance of content and compactness.
  • V2 — Compact Two-line (~70px): Title + truncated tagline on left, stats inline on right. Saves significant vertical space while keeping stats prominent.
  • V3 — Slim Banner (~50px): Everything on one line. Title, short tagline, and stats as compact chips. Maximum vertical space recovery — feels like a branded toolbar.
  • All use Filter V2 (Scrollable Pills) with horizontal scroll, count badges, and search input.
  • Previous hero was ~185px tall. V1 saves ~40%, V2 saves ~62%, V3 saves ~73%.
